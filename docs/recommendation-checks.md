# A short home test for Tastebud

Use the same favourite films when comparing this build with your previous copy. The useful outcome is a film you choose and enjoy. A convincing taste description alone does not establish a good recommendation.

## Try the personal flow first

| Check | What to do | What a useful result looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your normal list | Paste your usual favourites, including the full numbered list if you have one. Choose **Find me a film**. | Three distinct films you could plausibly enjoy, with recognisable connections to your taste. |
| Another round | Choose **Try three more**. | Fresh suggestions, with no repeat from the current session and no forced curveball. |
| Tonight’s mood | Add a short note such as “Something clever and funny; nothing too bleak.” | The old result clears. New picks respond to the note without changing your saved favourites. |
| Save for later | Create or load a profile and save one recommendation. | It appears in your watchlist. Saving does not count as having seen or liked it. |
| Viewing and enjoyment | Mark a film **Already seen**, then record **Liked it** or **Didn’t like it** when you have an opinion. | Viewing and preference stay distinct. Liking a film adds it to your favourites. |
| A temporary pass | Choose **Not tonight**. | The film is skipped for this session, without becoming a permanent dislike. |

## Then try it with someone

Open **With someone** and enter both people’s favourites. Use a few films that represent each person; longer lists are supported too.

- Do both people feel represented by the main pick?
- Does each explanation make a specific, credible connection to that viewer’s films?
- Do the two alternatives also look worthwhile?
- Does another round avoid earlier suggestions?
- Do you actually settle on a film and start it?

The labelled example is a layout sample. Use your existing OpenAI configuration for real personalised picks. This build supports two people in the shared flow.

## Jot down five things

1. How long it took for usable picks to appear.
2. Which picks you would actually watch, and why any others missed.
3. Any incorrect title or film detail, or an explanation that does not fit.
4. How long it took to choose a film, including any extra rounds.
5. Whether you watched it, enjoyed it and wanted to use Tastebud next time.

There are no active streaming-service or catalogue checks in this build. Check where to watch separately when you choose a film. The app does not verify generated film details.

Guest profiles last for the session. If you already use a signed-in account, also check that your saved profile and feedback remain after signing out and back in. Signing in does not import a guest profile.

Automated tests use mocked AI and account responses. They check the mechanics and feedback meanings; live credentials, authentication, response speed and subjective recommendation quality still need these practical checks.
